CineReserve – Smart Movie Reservation & Cinema Management

An original, production-style cinema booking platform built with Django, PostgreSQL/SQLite, REST APIs, transactional seat locking, simulated payments, QR/PDF tickets, and a Scikit-Learn recommendation engine.

Engineering

Role-based access, CSRF, hashed passwords, relational design, and ACID seat locks with select_for_update.

Booking workflow

Movie → cinema → date → showtime → seats → coupon → payment → QR ticket → history & refunds.

AIML

TF-IDF + cosine similarity recommendations from live bookings, wishlist, and genre preferences, plus a rule-based NLP chatbot.
