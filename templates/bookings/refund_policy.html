Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

1. Tiered Refund Timetable

CineReserve enforces an automated, time-tiered refund mechanism for customer convenience and auditorium seat inventory balance:

Cancellation Window Refund Rate Applicability
More than 24 Hours prior to Showtime 100% Ticket Refund Full ticket amount refunded to original payment method.
Between 2 and 24 Hours prior to Showtime 75% Ticket Refund 75% of base ticket cost refunded; 25% retained for seat allocation.
Less than 2 Hours prior to Showtime No Refund (0%) Late cancellations are non-refundable as auditoriums prepare for entry.
2. Processing Timelines

Approved refunds are credited directly back to the source payment method (UPI, Card, or Digital Wallet) within 3 to 5 banking business days.

3. Convenience Fees

Internet convenience fees and applicable statutory taxes levied during initial booking are non-refundable statutory processing fees.
