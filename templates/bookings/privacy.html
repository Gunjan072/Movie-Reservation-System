Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2026

1. Data Collected

We collect username, email address, mobile number, date of birth, and booking histories to facilitate movie ticket reservations and recommend tailored movie experiences.

2. Payment Security Guarantee

CineReserve does NOT store raw card details, credit card security numbers (CVV), or banking passwords. Transactions are processed via encrypted tokens complying with PCI-DSS standards.

3. Machine Learning Usage

Browsing behaviors, genres booked, and wishlist selections are processed by our TF-IDF and Cosine Similarity AI recommendation algorithms to tailor movie suggestions without third-party data tracking.
