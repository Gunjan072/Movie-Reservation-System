Everything you need to know about reserving tickets and managing reservations on CineReserve.

When you select your seats and click "Proceed to Payment", CineReserve initiates an atomic database lock using select_for_update(). Those seats are held exclusively for you for 5 minutes. If another customer attempts to select the same seats simultaneously, the system prevents double-booking and alerts them.
